Questions

Online Store, answered

Do I need a developer to set up my store?

No. You add your products in Griv. If you’d rather build your own storefront, see Griv for developers.

How do customers pay?

By bank transfer or card at checkout. Each payment is attached to its order.

What happens to my stock when someone orders?

It updates automatically, and the same count is used by your in-store Point of Sale.

What does it cost?

See the pricing page.
