Who we are

Griv (“Griv”, “we”, “us”) provides a platform that helps businesses collect and make payments, sell products, manage inventory and run their operations. The data controller for the purposes of this policy is [Griv legal entity name], registered at [registered address].

This policy explains how we handle personal information about the businesses that use Griv, the people who work for them, their customers, and visitors to this website.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Business details, such as your business name, address and registration information.
  • Information needed to verify your identity and your business, as required by law and by our partners.
  • Messages you send us, for example through our contact form.

Information created when you use Griv

  • Transaction records, such as payments, payouts, orders, invoices and refunds.
  • Information about your customers and products that you add to Griv.
  • Technical information, such as device and log data, used to keep the service working and secure.

How we use it

  • To provide and operate Griv, including processing payments and keeping records.
  • To verify identities and prevent fraud and financial crime.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.
  • To provide support and respond to your messages.
  • To improve Griv, including features such as Griv AI, which uses your business data to answer your questions.
