About Griv

Built for the businesses that keep Africa moving.

The fashion designer in Lekki. The phone shop at Computer Village. The caterer in Abuja who takes orders on WhatsApp. Griv exists to give every one of them the tools to run, manage and grow — in one place.

The problem

Running a business here means running a dozen systems — a bank app for transfers, a notebook for stock, a spreadsheet for sales, screenshots for proof of payment, and a long evening at the end of every month making it all agree.

Bank appNotebookPOS receiptsWhatsAppSpreadsheetInvoice templateInstagram DMsPayroll list

None of these tools are bad. They just don’t talk to each other — so the business owner becomes the connection between them.

Our approach

One connected platform.

Griv brings payments, commerce and operations together, so one action updates everything it should. When a customer pays, the order is created, the stock moves, and the books already know.

  1. A customer paysPayment link, transfer, card or POS
  2. The order is createdWith the customer and items attached
  3. Stock updatesOnline and in-store, one count
  4. The records reconcilePayment, order and settlement matched
  5. The owner understandsReports and answers from Griv AI

What we believe

The principles behind Griv

01

Small businesses deserve great software.

The tools big companies use to see their whole business shouldn’t be out of reach for a shop with three staff.

02

Built for how business really works here.

Transfers, chats, cash at the counter, customers who pay in parts. Griv is designed around African business realities — not adapted to them later.

03

Simple on the surface, serious underneath.

Easy enough for your first sale, solid enough to trust with your money and your records as you grow.

04

Connected beats more features.

A business doesn’t need another tool. It needs the tools it has to work together.

Where we’re going

Launching in Nigeria. Built for the world.

We’re starting with Nigerian businesses, in Naira, with the payment methods their customers already use. Our ambition is bigger: to be the platform businesses across Africa — and beyond — grow into.

Over time, Griv will also help businesses access financial services such as business credit. Start with what you need today. Grow with everything Griv can do tomorrow.
