What happens when you press Charge

  1. Payment

    Your customer pays by transfer or card and the payment is recorded against the sale.

  2. Inventory

    Stock for each item goes down — the same stock your online store sells from.

  3. Transactions

    The sale appears in your transaction history with its order number.

  4. Reporting

    Today’s sales update, so you can see how the shop is doing without counting the till.
