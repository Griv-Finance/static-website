    Questions

    Virtual accounts, answered

    Who holds the money in my virtual account?

    Griv is a financial technology company, not a bank. Collections are provided in partnership with licensed partner institutions, and customer funds are held with a partner bank. See our terms for details.

    Can my customers pay from any bank?

    Your customers can transfer to your account number from their own banking app. If you have a question about a specific bank, contact support.

    How do I know a transfer is from the right customer?

    Each transfer is recorded with the sender’s name, the amount, the time and a reference, so you can see who paid and what for.
