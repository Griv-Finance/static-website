Recommendations are suggestions. Actions are yours.

Griv AI makes it clear which things are suggestions and which have actually happened. It never moves money or changes your records on its own — every action is prepared for you to check, then carried out only when you confirm.

  • SuggestedGriv AI thinks this is worth doing. Nothing has changed yet.
