How it works

Questions

Payment links, answered

Does my customer need a Griv account to pay?

No. Your customer opens the link in their browser and pays by bank transfer or card. Only your business needs a Griv account.

Do I need a website to use payment links?

No. Payment links work on their own — you can share them in any chat, social post, text message or email.

Where does the money go?

Successful payments appear in your Griv balance with the customer, amount and reference, ready to use for payouts or to settle to your bank account.
