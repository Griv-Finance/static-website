Questions

Invoices, answered

How do my customers pay an invoice?

Each invoice includes a link to pay online. Your customer opens it and pays by bank transfer or card — no Griv account needed.

What happens when an invoice is paid?

The invoice is marked paid, the money appears in your Griv balance, and the payment is linked to the invoice in your transaction history.

Can I see which invoices are overdue?

Yes. Invoices that pass their due date without payment are shown as overdue in your invoice list.
