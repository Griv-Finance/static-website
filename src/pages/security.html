Our approach

How we look after your business

Licensed partners hold the money

Griv is a financial technology company, not a bank. Payment, collection and payout services are provided in partnership with licensed institutions, and customer funds are held with a partner bank.

You confirm what moves

Payouts and other actions that move money are reviewed and confirmed by you. Griv AI prepares suggestions — it never moves money on its own.

Records that reconcile

Every payment is tied to its order and settlement record, so anything that doesn’t add up is surfaced instead of hidden.

Security by design

We treat the protection of your money and data as part of building the product — not something added afterwards.

Protect your business

Simple habits that stop most fraud.

Most account takeovers start with someone being tricked into sharing a code or a password. These habits make that much harder.

  1. Never share one-time codes.No one from Griv will ask you for a password or a code — by phone, chat, email or in person.
  2. Check before you sign in.Make sure you’re on the real Griv site before entering your details. Be wary of links sent in messages.
  3. Confirm payment before you deliver.Check that a payment shows as successful in Griv — not just a screenshot or an alert someone forwards you.
  4. Use a strong, unique password.Don’t reuse the password from your email or social media accounts.
  5. Only give access to people who need it.Remove access for staff who no longer work with you.
