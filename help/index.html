Getting started

What is Griv?

Griv is a platform for businesses to collect and make payments, sell products, manage inventory and run their operations from one place. See all products.

How do I open a Griv account?

Choose Start for Free and tell us about your business. We’ll guide you through setting up.

Where is Griv available?

Griv is launching in Nigeria. Availability of products and features may vary by market.

How much does Griv cost?

Starting is free. See the pricing page for each product.

Getting paid

How do I create a payment link?

Create a link with a title and amount, then share it in any chat, post, message or email. More about payment links.

How can my customers pay?

Customers can pay by bank transfer or card through payment links, invoices and your online store, or transfer to your virtual account number.

How do I know an invoice has been paid?

Paid invoices are marked as paid in your invoice list, and the payment appears in your transaction history. More about invoices.

A customer says they’ve paid by transfer — how do I check?

Transfers to your virtual account appear with the sender’s name, amount and reference. More about virtual accounts.

Paying out

How do I pay a supplier?

Choose a beneficiary, enter the amount, review and confirm. More about payouts.

Can I pay my whole team at once?

Yes — bulk payouts let you pay many people with a single review and confirmation.

A payout failed. What now?

Failed payouts are shown in your payout history. Check the recipient details and try again, or contact support.

How do Griv cards work?

Griv business cards let your team pay for business purchases, with every spend recorded. Cards are issued by a licensed issuing partner. More about cards.

Selling

Do my shop and online store share stock?

Yes. Your Point of Sale and online store use the same inventory, so every sale updates one stock count. More about inventory.

How do I know when stock is running low?

Products that are running low are flagged in your inventory, and sold-out products are marked.

How do I set up an online store?

Add your products with prices and stock, and they’re ready to sell online. More about the online store.

Records & reports

What does reconciliation do?

It matches each payment to its order and settlement record, and flags payments that are unmatched or don’t agree. More about reconciliation.

What can I see in analytics?

Sales over time, where sales come from, your best-selling products and returning customers. More about analytics.

Can Griv AI make changes to my account?

Griv AI suggests actions and prepares them for you to review. Nothing happens until you confirm. More about Griv AI.

Account safety

Will Griv ever ask for my password or one-time code?

Never share your password or one-time codes with anyone — including people who say they work for Griv. See Security for more ways to stay safe.

I think someone has accessed my account.

Change your password straight away and contact support.
